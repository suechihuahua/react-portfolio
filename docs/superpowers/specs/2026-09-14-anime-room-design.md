# Anime Room Portfolio — Design

Date: 2026-09-14
Replaces the cyber solar system hero (archived under `archive/solar-system/`, tag `solar-system-v1`).

## 1. Goal

A door on black opens into an illustrated anime bedroom. The visitor's avatar (the user, drawn in the style of their portrait) is in the room; each portfolio section is a spot in the room and a pose: clicking the avatar zooms in, he changes into a suit and "speaks" the About me lines; the other sections move the camera to the bookshelf, desk, wall or bed with a matching pose and a content card.

Keep: résumé download, LinkedIn/emails footer, per-route titles, meta/OG, Vercel + analytics, Vitest suite, responsive 375/768/1440, no horizontal scroll.

## 2. Assets

All art is a single generated sheet `art/sheet.png` (1536×1024): a room panel on top (≈1536×440) and six pose panels below, each on a *painted* checkerboard. `scripts/slice-art.mjs` (Playwright canvas) produces `public/room/`:

- `room.jpg` — the room panel upscaled 2× (3072×880), JPEG q85.
- `pose-pc.png`, `pose-about.png`, `pose-education.png`, `pose-work.png`, `pose-skills.png`, `pose-hobbies.png` — panels cropped, checkerboard removed by flood-filling transparency from the panel edges over near-white/near-grey checker pixels, then trimmed to the character's bounding box. Each file records its trimmed offset in `public/room/poses.json` so placement is stable.

Panel rectangles live in the script as constants; re-run with `npm run art` if the sheet changes. If the sheet is missing, the script exits with a message and the app falls back to `public/room/placeholder-*.png` silhouettes (generated by the same script from simple shapes) so the site still runs.

## 3. Experience

### 3.1 Door intro (`/`, first visit per session)

- Black page. One weathered white six-panel door, drawn in CSS/SVG (no film stills), centred, slight vignette, dust motes. Caption "click to enter" fades in after 1.5 s.
- Click / Enter / tap: the door rotates open (CSS 3D, 1.1 s), warm light spills through, the camera dollies forward (scale + blur), then cross-fades into the room. Reduced motion: instant cut.
- Skipped on later navigations in the same session (`sessionStorage` flag) and for deep links to a section (the room appears directly, door skipped).

### 3.2 The room stage

- `RoomStage` is a fixed full-viewport element containing the room image (`object-fit: cover`), the avatar layer, ambient layers (monitor glow, window light, drifting dust), and hotspot buttons.
- A "camera" is a CSS transform on the stage (`scale`, `translate`) animated with framer-motion springs. Each spot defines `{ x, y, zoom }` in percentages of the room image, so it works at any viewport; on ≤768 px the zooms are 25 % tighter.
- Parallax: mouse/gyro moves the stage ±1.5 % opposite to the pointer; disabled on reduced motion.

### 3.3 Spots and poses

`site.js` gains, per section, `spot: { x, y, zoom }`, `pose`, `avatar: { x, y, height }` (where the pose sprite stands, in room %), and `lines: string[]` (dialogue). Sections and their actions:

| slug | label | spot | pose | dialogue gist |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| about | About me | on the avatar (centre-left) | suit | first-person intro from the résumé header |
| education | Education | bookshelf (centre) | reading | NTU BComp CS 2025–2029; TMJC A-levels |
| work | Work experience | desk edge (right) | lanyard + laptop | Supply Base East (NS), Tori-Q |
| projects | Projects | monitors (right) | typing at PC | Garena hackathon Roblox game; this site |
| skills | Skills | poster wall (top right) | leaning on wall | languages, digital skills |
| hobbies | Hobbies | bed (left) | controller | gym, basketball, anime/drama, gaming, building |

Idle (home, no section): pose `pc` at the desk, camera wide.

Navigation: HUD menu (six sections + Résumé), clicking a hotspot or the avatar, scroll/swipe/arrow keys stepping through sections in order (reuse `useScrollNavigation`), and URL routes `/<slug>`.

### 3.4 Dialogue + card

- On arriving at a spot: pose cross-fades (200 ms), camera settles (~700 ms), then a visual-novel dialogue box at the bottom types `lines` at 28 ms/char with a blinking caret; click advances/skips.
- Voice toggle in the HUD (off by default): when on, `speechSynthesis` reads each line; stops on navigation.
- After the last line (or immediately on skip) the content card slides in beside the avatar (right side on desktop, bottom sheet on mobile) with the structured content (reusing the existing prose/list/courses/projects renderers, plus a new `timeline` kind for education/work).

### 3.5 Tiers and fallbacks

- No WebGL needed. Reduced motion: no door animation, camera cuts, dialogue appears at once.
- Images: room JPEG ~400 kB and pose PNGs ~150 kB each, pose PNGs lazy-loaded (`loading="lazy"`, preloaded on hover of the menu). Poster/OG regenerated from the room stage.

## 4. Architecture

```
src/
  content/site.js            person, sections (spot/pose/lines/content), order
  components/
    DoorIntro.jsx            door + open animation, sets `entered`
    RoomStage.jsx            room image, ambient layers, avatar, hotspots, camera transform
    Avatar.jsx               pose sprite with cross-fade
    Dialogue.jsx             typed lines, skip, voice
    SectionCard.jsx          content card (renderers)
    HUD.jsx / Layout.jsx     menu, footer, routes → activeSlug
  store/useRoomStore.js      entered, activeSlug, dialogueDone, voiceOn, reducedMotion
  hooks/useScrollNavigation.js (kept), useDocumentTitle.js (kept), useSpeech.js
scripts/slice-art.mjs, capture.mjs (updated), screenshots.mjs (kept)
public/room/*
```

Removed: `src/components/three/**`, `useSceneStore`, `renderTier`, `StaticHero`, `SceneErrorBoundary`, `vendor-three` chunking and `check-bundle`'s three.js assertions (replaced by a check that the initial chunk stays under 80 kB gzip). three/drei/postprocessing/maath/gsap dependencies removed.

## 5. Testing

- `slice-art` unit: flood-fill keying on a synthetic checkerboard + coloured blob leaves the blob opaque and the border transparent (pure function tested in node with a small pixel array).
- Store/route tests: every section route renders its card heading; `/` shows the door on first visit and skips it when `sessionStorage` says entered; deep link `/about` skips the door.
- Dialogue: types the lines, skip shows the card, voice toggle calls `speechSynthesis.speak` when on.
- Camera: `getCameraTransform(spot, viewport)` pure function tested at 375/768/1440.
- Screenshots at three widths for `/`, `/about`, `/hobbies`; Lighthouse mobile ≥ 85 on `/`.
